More than a Stay,
A Local Memory

The Urban Homestay was built for the traveler who seeks the privacy of a hotel but the heart of a home. Located as a central gateway in Dibrugarh, we connect you to the heritage of the tea gardens and the vastness of the Brahmaputra, all while providing a boutique haven to return to.
